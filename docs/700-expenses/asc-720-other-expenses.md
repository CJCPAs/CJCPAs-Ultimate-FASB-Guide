# ASC 720: Other Expenses

> Guidance on advertising costs, start-up activities, contributions, and other specific expense topics.

## Overview

ASC 720 addresses accounting for various expense categories that don't fit elsewhere in the Codification, including advertising costs, start-up activities, insurance costs, contributions made, and real and personal property taxes.

> **Core Principle:** Most costs covered by ASC 720 should be expensed as incurred, with limited exceptions for direct-response advertising that meets specific capitalization criteria.

---

## Scope and Subtopics

| Subtopic | Title | Key Guidance |
|----------|-------|--------------|
| ASC 720-15 | Start-Up Costs | Expense as incurred |
| ASC 720-25 | Contributions Made | Expense when made |
| ASC 720-30 | Real and Personal Property Taxes | Accrue ratably |
| ASC 720-35 | Advertising Costs | Generally expense as incurred |
| ASC 720-45 | Business and Technology Reengineering | Expense as incurred |
| ASC 720-50 | Web Site Development | Capitalize certain costs |

## ASC 720-35: Advertising Costs

### General Rule

**Expense advertising costs as incurred** or when advertising first takes place.

### Exceptions—Direct-Response Advertising

May capitalize if **ALL** criteria are met:

| Criterion | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Primary purpose** | Elicit sales to customers who respond specifically |
| **Response mechanism** | Customer responds through identifiable means (code, coupon) |
| **Probable future benefits** | Demonstrates history of reliable results |
| **Reliably measurable** | Results can be tracked to specific campaigns |

### Types of Advertising Costs

| Type | Treatment |
|------|-----------|
| Media placement (TV, print, digital) | Expense as incurred |
| Production costs | Expense when first aired/published |
| Catalog costs | Expense when mailed (or capitalize if direct-response criteria met) |
| Cooperative advertising | Reduce revenue or expense |
| Celebrity endorsements | Expense over endorsement period |

### Journal Entries

**General Advertising (expensed as incurred):**
```
Dr. Advertising Expense                  $100,000
    Cr. Accounts Payable                        $100,000
```

**Production Costs (expensed when first used):**
```
At production:
Dr. Prepaid Advertising                   $50,000
    Cr. Cash                                     $50,000

When commercial first airs:
Dr. Advertising Expense                   $50,000
    Cr. Prepaid Advertising                      $50,000
```

**Direct-Response Advertising (if criteria met):**
```
Dr. Deferred Advertising Costs            $75,000
    Cr. Cash                                     $75,000

Amortization (over period of expected benefit):
Dr. Advertising Expense                   $25,000
    Cr. Deferred Advertising Costs               $25,000
```

## ASC 720-15: Start-Up Costs

### Definition

Costs incurred for:
- One-time activities for opening a new facility
- Introducing new products or services
- Conducting business in new territory
- Initiating new processes
- Starting new operations
- Organizing a new entity

### Accounting Treatment

**Expense all start-up costs as incurred**—no capitalization permitted.

### Examples of Start-Up Costs

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Opening costs** | Pre-opening advertising, employee training |
| **Organization costs** | Legal fees for incorporation, state fees |
| **New location** | Relocation costs, new market research |
| **New product launch** | Pre-launch marketing, test market costs |

### What Is NOT a Start-Up Cost

| Item | Proper Treatment |
|------|------------------|
| Property, plant, equipment | Capitalize per ASC 360 |
| Inventory | Capitalize per ASC 330 |
| Intangible assets | Capitalize if acquired |
| Debt issuance costs | Offset against debt |
| R&D costs | Expense per ASC 730 |

### Journal Entry

**Scenario:** Company incurs $200,000 in pre-opening costs for new store.

```
Dr. Start-Up Costs Expense               $200,000
    Cr. Cash/Accounts Payable                   $200,000
```

## ASC 720-50: Website Development Costs

### Development Stages

| Stage | Activities | Treatment |
|-------|------------|-----------|
| **Planning** | Conceptual design, vendor selection | Expense |
| **Application Development** | Coding, software configuration | Capitalize |
| **Graphics Development** | Design of overall look | Capitalize |
| **Content Development** | Creating text, links (not database) | Expense |
| **Operating** | Training, maintenance | Expense |

### Capitalization Requirements

Capitalize costs incurred during application and infrastructure development stage if:
- Website will be used internally, OR
- Website will be used to sell goods/services

### Amortization

- Amortize over useful life (typically 2-5 years)
- Begin when website is ready for intended use

### Journal Entries

**Capitalizable Development Costs:**
```
Dr. Website Development Costs            $150,000
    Cr. Cash                                    $150,000
```

**Amortization (assuming 3-year life):**
```
Dr. Amortization Expense                  $50,000
    Cr. Accumulated Amortization                 $50,000
```

## ASC 720-25: Contributions Made

### Recognition

**Expense contributions when made** (unconditional) or when conditions are substantially met (conditional).

### Measurement

| Type | Measurement |
|------|-------------|
| Cash contributions | Amount given |
| Noncash contributions | Fair value at date of gift |
| Services | Fair value if would be purchased |

### Conditional Contributions

If contribution includes conditions (barriers and right of return):
- Recognize expense when conditions are substantially met
- Until then, record as prepaid asset

### Journal Entries

**Unconditional Cash Contribution:**
```
Dr. Contribution Expense                 $10,000
    Cr. Cash                                    $10,000
```

**Contribution of Property:**
```
Dr. Contribution Expense (at FV)         $50,000
Dr. Accumulated Depreciation             $30,000
    Cr. Property (at cost)                      $75,000
    Cr. Gain on Disposal                         $5,000
```

## ASC 720-30: Real and Personal Property Taxes

### Recognition

Accrue property taxes **ratably over the fiscal period** of the taxing authority.

### Methods

| Method | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Proration** | Allocate annual tax over months |
| **Specific identification** | If paid in advance or arrears |

### Example

**Scenario:** Annual property tax of $120,000 for calendar year, due June 30.

**Monthly Accrual:**
```
Dr. Property Tax Expense                  $10,000
    Cr. Property Tax Payable                    $10,000
```

**Payment on June 30:**
```
Dr. Property Tax Payable                  $60,000
Dr. Prepaid Property Tax                  $60,000
    Cr. Cash                                    $120,000
```

## ASC 720-45: Business and Technology Reengineering

### General Rule

**Expense as incurred** unless costs meet capitalization criteria under other standards.

### Common Costs

| Cost | Treatment |
|------|-----------|
| Consulting fees | Expense |
| Internal labor | Expense |
| Training | Expense |
| Software (if purchased) | May capitalize per ASC 350 |
| Hardware | Capitalize per ASC 360 |

## Practical Application

### Advertising Budget Example

**Annual Advertising Spending:**

| Category | Amount | Treatment |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| TV production | $500,000 | Defer until first airing |
| Media placement | $2,000,000 | Expense as incurred |
| Print ads | $300,000 | Expense when published |
| Digital campaigns | $800,000 | Expense as incurred |
| Direct mail (qualifies) | $400,000 | Capitalize, amortize |
| **Total** | **$4,000,000** | |

### Start-Up Cost Analysis

**New Restaurant Opening:**

| Cost | Amount | Treatment |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Leasehold improvements | $500,000 | Capitalize (ASC 360) |
| Equipment | $200,000 | Capitalize (ASC 360) |
| Initial inventory | $50,000 | Capitalize (ASC 330) |
| Pre-opening wages | $75,000 | Expense (start-up) |
| Pre-opening advertising | $25,000 | Expense (start-up) |
| Training costs | $30,000 | Expense (start-up) |
| Grand opening event | $20,000 | Expense (start-up) |

## Disclosure Requirements

### Advertising Costs

- Total advertising expense for period
- Policy for recognizing advertising costs
- Amount of capitalized direct-response advertising

### Start-Up Costs

- Disclose if material amounts expensed

### Example Disclosures

> **Advertising:** The Company expenses advertising costs as incurred. Advertising expense was $X million for the year ended December 31, 20XX.

> **Start-Up Costs:** Costs associated with opening new stores are expensed as incurred. Pre-opening costs totaled $X million for the year ended December 31, 20XX.

## Private Company Considerations

### Practical Approaches

- Same standards apply to private companies
- Often simpler advertising arrangements
- May have fewer direct-response situations

### Common Issues

1. Capitalizing general advertising inappropriately
2. Capitalizing start-up costs
3. Not accruing property taxes monthly
4. Improper treatment of pre-opening costs

---

## Common Audit Issues

1. **Direct-response criteria** — Not properly evaluated
2. **Start-up vs. capital** — Costs improperly capitalized
3. **Timing** — Advertising expensed too early/late
4. **Property tax accruals** — Not recorded ratably
5. **Website costs** — Wrong stage classification
6. **Cooperative advertising** — Not properly offset against revenue
7. **Amortization** — Capitalized costs not amortized properly

---

## GAAP vs. Tax Differences

| Item | GAAP | Tax |
|------|------|-----|
| Start-up costs | Expense immediately | May elect to amortize over 15 years |
| Advertising | Mostly expense | Same (with exceptions) |
| Organization costs | Expense immediately | May elect to amortize |

Creates deferred tax considerations when GAAP and tax treatment differ.
