# ASC 225: Income Statement

> The authoritative guide to reporting an entity's operating performance and results.

## Overview

ASC 225 provides guidance on the presentation of income and expenses in the income statement (statement of operations). It addresses the classification of items, extraordinary items (now eliminated), unusual items, and the reporting of expenses by nature or function. The income statement is a critical financial statement showing an entity's profitability over a period.

**Core Principle:**
> "The income statement reports the results of operations for a period, presenting revenues, expenses, gains, and losses in a manner that facilitates analysis of the entity's operating performance."

---

## Scope

### Applies To:
- All entities presenting an income statement in conformity with U.S. GAAP
- Classification of revenues, expenses, gains, and losses
- Presentation of operating vs. non-operating items
- Reporting of unusual or infrequent items

### Does NOT Apply To:
- Comprehensive income presentation (ASC 220)
- Earnings per share (ASC 260)
- Segment reporting (ASC 280)
- Industry-specific presentation (various ASC 900 topics)

---

## Income Statement Structure

### Basic Components

| Component | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Revenues** | Inflows from primary operations (sales, services, fees) |
| **Cost of Revenue** | Direct costs of generating revenue |
| **Gross Profit** | Revenues less cost of revenue |
| **Operating Expenses** | SG&A, R&D, depreciation, other operating costs |
| **Operating Income** | Gross profit less operating expenses |
| **Other Income/Expense** | Non-operating items (interest, gains/losses) |
| **Income Before Tax** | Operating income plus/minus other items |
| **Income Tax Expense** | Current and deferred tax provisions |
| **Net Income** | Bottom line profit or loss |

---

## Presentation Formats

### Single-Step Income Statement

All revenues and gains grouped together, all expenses and losses grouped together:

```
COMPANY XYZ
Income Statement
For the Year Ended December 31, 20X1

Revenues and gains:
  Net sales                              $2,500,000
  Service revenue                           400,000
  Interest income                            15,000
  Gain on sale of equipment                  25,000
                                         ----------
Total revenues and gains                  2,940,000

Expenses and losses:
  Cost of goods sold                      1,500,000
  Selling expenses                          300,000
  General and administrative                400,000
  Interest expense                           50,000
  Income tax expense                        172,500
                                         ----------
Total expenses and losses                 2,422,500
                                         ----------
Net income                               $  517,500
                                         ==========
```

---

### Multiple-Step Income Statement

Separates operating from non-operating items with intermediate subtotals:

```
COMPANY XYZ
Income Statement
For the Year Ended December 31, 20X1

Net sales                                           $2,500,000
Cost of goods sold                                   1,500,000
                                                    ----------
Gross profit                                         1,000,000

Operating expenses:
  Selling expenses                      $  300,000
  General and administrative               400,000
  Research and development                 100,000
                                        ----------
Total operating expenses                               800,000
                                                    ----------
Operating income                                       200,000

Other income (expense):
  Interest income                           15,000
  Interest expense                         (50,000)
  Gain on sale of equipment                 25,000
                                        ----------
Total other income (expense)                           (10,000)
                                                    ----------
Income before income taxes                             190,000
Income tax expense                                      47,500
                                                    ----------
Net income                                          $  142,500
                                                    ==========
```

## Classification of Items

### Operating vs. Non-Operating

| Category | Examples | Treatment |
|----------|----------|-----------|
| **Operating revenues** | Sales, service fees, royalties | Above operating income line |
| **Cost of revenue** | COGS, cost of services | Directly after revenue |
| **Operating expenses** | SG&A, R&D, depreciation | Within operating section |
| **Other income** | Interest income, dividend income | Below operating income |
| **Other expenses** | Interest expense, other financing costs | Below operating income |
| **Gains** | Asset sales, lawsuit settlements | Other income or separate |
| **Losses** | Asset impairments, lawsuit losses | Other expense or separate |

### Nature vs. Function Classification

**Function Method (Most Common in U.S.):**
```
Cost of goods sold                $1,500,000
Selling expenses                     300,000
General and administrative           400,000
Research and development             100,000
```

**Nature Method:**
```
Materials and supplies used       $  800,000
Employee compensation               900,000
Depreciation expense                200,000
Rent expense                        150,000
Utilities                            50,000
Professional services               100,000
Other expenses                      100,000
```

## Unusual and Infrequent Items

### Background—Elimination of Extraordinary Items

**Historical Context:** Prior to ASU 2015-01, extraordinary items were reported separately, net of tax. This classification was eliminated for fiscal years beginning after December 15, 2015.

### Current Requirements

Unusual or infrequently occurring items should be:
- Reported as **separate line items** within continuing operations
- Disclosed as to their **nature and financial effects**
- **NOT** presented net of tax

### Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Unusual** | High degree of abnormality; unrelated or only incidentally related to ordinary activities |
| **Infrequent** | Not reasonably expected to recur in the foreseeable future |

### Examples of Unusual/Infrequent Items

| Item | Classification | Rationale |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Major lawsuit settlement | Unusual | Not normal operations |
| Natural disaster loss | Infrequent | Rare occurrence |
| Restructuring charges | May be unusual | Depends on frequency |
| Goodwill impairment | Separate line | Significant judgment |
| Gain on sale of business | Separate line | Material transaction |

### Presentation

```
Operating income                                    $500,000

Other income (expense):
  Interest expense                                  (75,000)
  Gain on sale of investments                        25,000
  Restructuring charges (Note X)                   (150,000)
  Hurricane damage loss (Note Y)                   (200,000)
                                                   --------
Total other expense                                (400,000)
                                                   --------
Income before income taxes                         $100,000
```

## Discontinued Operations (ASC 205-20)

### Criteria for Discontinued Operations

A component qualifies as discontinued operations if:
1. **Disposed of** (or classified as held for sale), AND
2. Represents a **strategic shift** that has (or will have) a major effect on operations and financial results

### Strategic Shift Examples

| Qualifies | May Not Qualify |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Disposal of a major geographic area | Sale of minor product line |
| Disposal of a major line of business | Routine asset dispositions |
| Disposal of a major equity method investment | Partial sale of subsidiary |

### Presentation Requirements

Discontinued operations are presented separately, **net of tax**:

```
Income from continuing operations                  $300,000

Discontinued operations:
  Loss from operations of discontinued
    component (including loss on disposal)        (180,000)
  Income tax benefit                                45,000
                                                  --------
  Loss from discontinued operations               (135,000)
                                                  --------
Net income                                        $165,000
```

## Specific Line Item Guidance

### Revenue Presentation

| Consideration | Guidance |
|---------------|----------|
| **Gross vs. Net** | Principal = gross; Agent = net (ASC 606) |
| **Disaggregation** | By type, geography, timing, contract type |
| **Returns and allowances** | Net against revenue or separate line |
| **Excise taxes collected** | Policy election—gross or net |

### Cost of Goods Sold

**Components typically include:**
- Direct materials
- Direct labor
- Manufacturing overhead (applied)
- Inventory adjustments (LCM, obsolescence)
- Inbound freight

**Excluded from COGS:**
- Selling costs
- General administrative costs
- Distribution costs (may vary)

### Selling, General, and Administrative (SG&A)

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Selling expenses** | Advertising, commissions, shipping out, sales salaries |
| **General expenses** | Executive salaries, legal, accounting, office supplies |
| **Administrative** | HR, IT, corporate overhead |

### Research and Development

Per ASC 730:
- Generally expensed as incurred
- May be presented as separate line or within operating expenses
- Disclose total R&D expense

### Depreciation and Amortization

**Presentation options:**
- Included within functional categories (COGS, SG&A)
- Separate line item on income statement
- Disclosed in notes if not on face

---

## Interest and Financing Costs

### Interest Expense

| Component | Treatment |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Interest on debt** | Period expense (unless capitalized) |
| **Amortization of debt issuance costs** | Interest expense |
| **Amortization of bond premium/discount** | Adjustment to interest |
| **Finance lease interest** | Interest expense (ASC 842) |

### Interest Income

- Generally presented in other income
- Financial institutions may present in revenues
- Disclose significant components

---

## Income Tax Presentation

### Basic Presentation

```
Income before income taxes                         $500,000
Income tax expense                                  125,000
                                                   --------
Net income                                         $375,000
```

### Components Disclosure

Disclose in notes:
- Current tax expense (federal, state, foreign)
- Deferred tax expense
- Rate reconciliation

## Practical Examples

### Example 1: Multiple-Step with Unusual Items

**Facts:**
- Net sales: $5,000,000
- COGS: $3,000,000
- SG&A: $800,000
- R&D: $200,000
- Interest expense: $100,000
- Restructuring charges: $150,000 (plant closure)
- Gain on sale of building: $75,000
- Tax rate: 25%

**Income Statement:**

```
COMPANY XYZ
Income Statement
For the Year Ended December 31, 20X1

Net sales                                          $5,000,000
Cost of goods sold                                  3,000,000
                                                   ----------
Gross profit                                        2,000,000

Operating expenses:
  Selling, general, and administrative   $800,000
  Research and development                200,000
                                        ---------
Total operating expenses                            1,000,000
                                                   ----------
Operating income                                    1,000,000

Other income (expense):
  Interest expense                       (100,000)
  Gain on sale of building                 75,000
  Restructuring charges                  (150,000)
                                        ---------
Total other expense                                  (175,000)
                                                   ----------
Income before income taxes                            825,000
Income tax expense                                    206,250
                                                   ----------
Net income                                        $   618,750
                                                   ==========
```

---

### Example 2: With Discontinued Operations

**Facts (same as above, plus):**
- Sold Widget Division (strategic shift)
- Widget Division operating loss: $200,000
- Loss on disposal of Widget Division: $100,000

**Income Statement:**

```
COMPANY XYZ
Income Statement
For the Year Ended December 31, 20X1

[Continuing operations - same as above through:]
Income from continuing operations                  $   618,750

Discontinued operations:
  Loss from Widget Division operations   $(200,000)
  Loss on disposal of Widget Division     (100,000)
                                         ---------
  Loss from discontinued operations,
    before income tax benefit             (300,000)
  Income tax benefit                        75,000
                                         ---------
  Loss from discontinued operations                   (225,000)
                                                   ----------
Net income                                        $   393,750
                                                   ==========
```

### Example 3: Journal Entries for Income Statement Items

**Recording sales with returns:**
```
Dr. Accounts Receivable              $100,000
    Cr. Sales Revenue                          $100,000

Dr. Sales Returns and Allowances     $5,000
    Cr. Accounts Receivable                    $5,000
(Or credit Refund Liability under ASC 606)
```

**Recording restructuring charges:**
```
Dr. Restructuring Charges            $150,000
    Cr. Accrued Restructuring Liability        $100,000
    Cr. Accumulated Depreciation                $50,000
```

**Recording income tax provision:**
```
Dr. Income Tax Expense               $206,250
    Cr. Income Tax Payable                     $180,000
    Cr. Deferred Tax Liability                  $26,250
```

## Private Company Considerations

### Simplified Presentation

Private companies may use:
- Single-step format (simpler)
- Combined line items (if not misleading)
- Less disaggregation (fewer segments)

### Bank Covenant Considerations

| Metric | Importance |
|--------|------------|
| **EBITDA** | Often used in covenants—clarify calculation |
| **Operating income** | May need specific definition |
| **Adjusted measures** | Document approved adjustments |

### Common Private Company Formats

Many private companies include:
- Officers' salaries as separate line
- Related party transactions identified
- Add-backs for owner compensation

## Common Audit Issues

### Classification Errors

| Error | Impact |
|-------|--------|
| Operating vs. non-operating | Distorts operating metrics |
| Revenue gross-up | Overstates revenues |
| Expense capitalization | Understates current expenses |
| Discontinued operations criteria | Wrong presentation |

### Disclosure Deficiencies

| Issue | Requirement |
|-------|-------------|
| Missing unusual item disclosure | Nature and amount required |
| Incomplete tax disclosure | Rate reconciliation needed |
| No discontinued operations notes | Detailed disclosure required |

### Materiality Considerations

- Individual line item materiality
- Cumulative error impact
- Qualitative factors (trends, covenants)

---

## Disclosure Requirements

### Required Disclosures

| Item | Disclosure |
|------|------------|
| **Accounting policies** | Revenue recognition, expense classification |
| **Unusual items** | Nature, amount, and impact |
| **Restructuring** | Detailed rollforward and nature |
| **Discontinued operations** | Comprehensive disclosure (ASC 205-20) |
| **Income taxes** | Components, rate reconciliation |

### Encouraged Disclosures

- Key performance metrics
- Operating expense detail
- Revenue disaggregation beyond requirements

---

## Comparative Statements

### Requirements

- Prior period(s) should be presented for comparison
- Restate for discontinued operations
- Restate for accounting changes (when required)
- Consistency in classification

### Reclassifications

When reclassifying prior periods:
- Disclose nature and reason
- Disclose amounts reclassified
- Ensure comparability
