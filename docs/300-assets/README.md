## Key Standards

### ASC 326: Credit Losses (CECL)

📌 **Major Standard — Critical for loan portfolios, receivables, and debt securities**

See detailed guide: [ASC 326: Credit Losses (CECL)](asc-326-credit-losses.md)

**Overview:**

The Current Expected Credit Loss (CECL) model requires entities to recognize lifetime expected credit losses immediately rather than waiting for a loss to be probable (incurred loss model).

**Scope:**
- Financial assets measured at amortized cost (loans, receivables, HTM securities)
- Off-balance sheet credit exposures (loan commitments, guarantees)
- Net investments in leases (lessor)

**Key Principles:**
1. Measure expected credit losses on a collective (pool) basis when similar risk characteristics exist
2. Consider historical loss experience, current conditions, and reasonable/supportable forecasts
3. Revert to historical loss information beyond the forecast period

**Effective Dates:**

| Entity Type | Effective Date |
|-------------|----------------|
| SEC filers (excluding SRCs) | Fiscal years beginning after 12/15/2019 |
| All other public entities | Fiscal years beginning after 12/15/2020 |
| Private companies & nonprofits | Fiscal years beginning after 12/15/2022 |

### ASC 330: Inventory

📌 **Major Standard — Core inventory accounting principles**

See detailed guide: [ASC 330: Inventory](asc-330-inventory.md)

**Cost Flow Assumptions:**
- FIFO (First-In, First-Out)
- LIFO (Last-In, First-Out) — US GAAP only
- Weighted Average
- Specific Identification

**Lower of Cost or Net Realizable Value (NRV):**

| Inventory Method | Measurement |
|------------------|-------------|
| Non-LIFO methods | Lower of cost or NRV |
| LIFO or retail inventory | Lower of cost or market |

**NRV** = Estimated selling price - Costs to complete and sell

### ASC 350: Intangibles—Goodwill and Other

📌 **Major Standard — Goodwill and intangible asset accounting**

See detailed guide: [ASC 350: Intangibles—Goodwill and Other](asc-350-intangibles-goodwill.md)

**Goodwill Impairment Testing:**

**For Public Business Entities:**
- One-step quantitative test: Compare fair value of reporting unit to carrying amount
- If carrying amount exceeds fair value, recognize impairment (limited to goodwill balance)
- Optional qualitative assessment first

**For Private Companies (PCC Alternative):**
- Can elect to amortize goodwill straight-line over 10 years (or less if appropriate)
- Simplified impairment test: Only test when triggering event occurs

**Indefinite-Lived Intangibles:**
- Not amortized
- Test for impairment annually (or more frequently if events indicate)

---

### ASC 360: Property, Plant, and Equipment

📌 **Major Standard — PP&E accounting and impairment**

See detailed guide: [ASC 360: Property, Plant, and Equipment](asc-360-ppe.md)

**Depreciation:**
- Systematic and rational allocation over useful life
- Methods: Straight-line, declining balance, units of production

**Impairment Testing (Asset Groups):**
- Test when events indicate carrying amount may not be recoverable
- Step 1: Recoverability test (compare carrying amount to undiscounted cash flows)
- Step 2: If not recoverable, measure impairment (carrying amount vs. fair value)

**Assets Held for Sale:**
- Classify separately on balance sheet
- Measure at lower of carrying amount or fair value less costs to sell
- Cease depreciation

## Practical Examples

### Inventory Write-Down

**Scenario:** Company has inventory with cost of $100,000. Due to market conditions, NRV is $85,000.

**Journal Entry:**
```
Dr. Loss on Inventory Write-Down    $15,000
    Cr. Inventory                           $15,000
```

### Goodwill Impairment (Private Company)

**Scenario:** Private company adopted PCC alternative. Goodwill of $500,000, amortizing over 10 years. Triggering event occurs in Year 3.

**Annual Amortization:**
```
Dr. Amortization Expense            $50,000
    Cr. Accumulated Amortization            $50,000
```

## Common Audit Issues

1. **Inventory Obsolescence** — Inadequate reserves for slow-moving items
2. **Goodwill Triggering Events** — Failure to identify indicators of impairment
3. **CECL Implementation** — Insufficient documentation of methodology
4. **Depreciation** — Lives not reassessed as circumstances change
