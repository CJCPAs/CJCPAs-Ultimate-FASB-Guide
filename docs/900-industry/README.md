## Key Industry Standards

### ASC 954: Health Care Entities

📌 **Major Standard — Detailed guide available:** [ASC 954: Health Care Entities](asc-954-healthcare-entities.md)

**Key Topics:**

| Area | Guidance |
|------|----------|
| **Charity Care** | Disclose policy, level, cost; not reported as revenue or receivable |
| **Third-Party Settlements** | Estimate amounts due to/from payors |
| **Malpractice Claims** | Accrue when probable and estimable |
| **Contributions** | Apply ASC 958 guidance |

**Plain English:** Healthcare organizations don't record charity care as revenue—they simply don't bill for it. They need to estimate and accrue settlements with Medicare, Medicaid, and insurers.

**Recent Focus:** Provider Relief Funds and COVID-related considerations

---

### ASC 958: Not-for-Profit Entities

📌 **Critical for nonprofits and their auditors — Detailed guide available:** [ASC 958: Not-for-Profit Entities](asc-958-not-for-profit.md)

**Key Concepts:**

**Net Asset Classification:**

| Class | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Without Donor Restrictions** | Available for any purpose |
| **With Donor Restrictions** | Subject to donor-imposed stipulations |

**Contribution Revenue:**
- Recognize when unconditional promise is received
- Conditional promises: Recognize when conditions are substantially met

**Distinguishing Contributions from Exchange Transactions:**
- **Contribution:** Donor receives no commensurate value (charitable intent)
- **Exchange:** Recipient provides goods/services of commensurate value

**Functional Expense Presentation:**
- Present expenses by nature and function
- Allocate joint costs between program and supporting services

### ASC 944: Financial Services—Insurance

**Insurance Contract Accounting:**

| Type | Measurement |
|------|-------------|
| Short-duration | Unearned premium liability |
| Long-duration | Liability for future policy benefits |

**Recent Update: ASU 2018-12 (LDTI)**
- Long-duration targeted improvements
- Changes to liability measurement, market risk benefits, DAC
- Effective: 2025 for SEC filers (other than SRCs), 2027 for others

---

### ASC 946: Financial Services—Investment Companies

**Key Features:**
- Report investments at fair value
- Recognize investment income and changes in fair value in operations
- Specific financial statement presentation requirements

**Scope:** Entities meeting investment company criteria in ASC 946-10-15

---

### ASC 970: Real Estate—General

**Key Topics:**

**Revenue Recognition for Real Estate Sales:**
- Apply ASC 606 unless within ASC 610-20 scope
- Full accrual method when control transfers
- Consider collectibility of sales price

**Capitalization of Costs:**
- Interest during development (ASC 835-20)
- Property taxes during development
- Pre-acquisition costs (if probable acquisition)

---

### ASC 985: Software

📌 **Major Standard — Detailed guide available:** [ASC 985: Software](asc-985-software.md)

**ASC 985-20: Costs of Software to Be Sold, Leased, or Marketed**

| Phase | Treatment |
|-------|-----------|
| Before technological feasibility | Expense (R&D) |
| After technological feasibility, before general release | Capitalize |
| After general release | Expense (maintenance) |

**Technological Feasibility Established When:**
- Working model completed (coding and testing), OR
- Detail program design completed

**Amortization:**
- Greater of: Straight-line over useful life OR ratio of current revenues to anticipated total revenues

## Practical Examples

### Not-for-Profit Contribution

**Scenario:** Foundation receives $100,000 grant restricted for building construction.

**At Receipt:**
```
Dr. Cash                         $100,000
    Cr. Contributions With Donor 
        Restrictions                     $100,000
```

**When Restriction Is Met (building completed):**
```
Dr. Net Assets With Donor 
    Restrictions                 $100,000
    Cr. Net Assets Without Donor
        Restrictions                     $100,000
```

### Healthcare Charity Care

**Scenario:** Hospital provides $500,000 of services (at gross charges) as charity care.

**Journal Entry:** None for revenue recognition (charity care is never billed)

**Disclosure Required:**
- Policy for providing charity care
- Level of charity care provided (preferably at cost)

### Software Development

**Scenario:** Software company incurs $200,000 before technological feasibility and $300,000 after.

**Before Technological Feasibility:**
```
Dr. R&D Expense                  $200,000
    Cr. Cash                             $200,000
```

**After Technological Feasibility:**
```
Dr. Software Development Costs   $300,000
    Cr. Cash                             $300,000
```

## Common Audit Issues by Industry

### Healthcare
1. Third-party settlements not properly estimated
2. Charity care mixed with bad debt
3. Provider relief fund accounting

### Not-for-Profit
1. Conditional vs. unconditional contributions
2. Exchange vs. contribution misclassification
3. Functional expense allocation methodology

### Real Estate
1. Revenue recognition timing
2. Interest capitalization calculations
3. Impairment of long-lived assets

### Software
1. Technological feasibility determination
2. Revenue recognition for multiple-element arrangements
3. Impairment of capitalized software costs

## Disclosure Requirements

### Not-for-Profit (ASC 958)

1. **Liquidity and availability** — Financial assets available within one year
2. **Functional expenses** — By nature and function
3. **Contributed nonfinancial assets** — Disaggregated by category (ASU 2020-07)
4. **Board designations** — Amounts and purposes

### Healthcare (ASC 954)

1. **Charity care** — Policy, level, method of estimating cost
2. **Third-party settlements** — Amounts due to/from payors
3. **Malpractice** — Nature, amounts accrued, range of possible additional loss
