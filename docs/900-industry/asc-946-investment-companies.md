# ASC 946: Financial Services—Investment Companies

> Specialized accounting guidance for entities that pool investor funds to invest in securities and other assets.

## Overview

ASC 946 provides accounting guidance specific to investment companies, including mutual funds, closed-end funds, business development companies (BDCs), and private equity/venture capital funds. Investment companies have unique financial statement presentation and measurement requirements, with most investments measured at fair value with changes recognized in earnings.

**Core Principle:**
> "Investment companies measure substantially all investments at fair value and report changes in fair value in the statement of operations. Financial statements are designed to provide investors with information relevant to evaluating the fund's performance and net asset value."

---

## Scope

### Investment Company Definition

An entity is an **investment company** if it meets ALL of the following:

| Criterion | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **1. Business purpose** | Obtains funds from investors for investing |
| **2. Commitment** | Commits to investors that its business purpose is investing for returns |
| **3. Pooling** | Obtains funds from multiple investors |
| **4. Ownership interests** | Has investors with ownership interests (e.g., shares, units) |
| **5. Management** | Substantially all investments managed on fair value basis |

### Typical Investment Characteristics (Not Required)

- Multiple investments
- Multiple investors
- Investors not related to sponsor
- Ownership in form of equity or partnership interests
- Proportionate share economics

### Does NOT Apply To:

- Operating companies
- Entities that fail investment company definition
- Employee benefit plans (ASC 960/962/965)
- REITs not meeting investment company criteria

## Types of Investment Companies

### Registered Investment Companies (RICs)

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Open-end funds (mutual funds)** | Continuously offer/redeem shares at NAV |
| **Closed-end funds** | Fixed number of shares; trade on exchange |
| **ETFs** | Exchange-traded; creation/redemption mechanism |
| **UITs** | Fixed portfolio; self-liquidating |

### Non-Registered Investment Companies

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Private equity funds** | Invest in operating companies |
| **Venture capital funds** | Invest in early-stage companies |
| **Hedge funds** | Various strategies; accredited investors |
| **Business development companies** | SEC-registered; lend to/invest in SMEs |
| **Fund of funds** | Invest in other investment companies |

## Financial Statement Presentation

### Required Financial Statements

1. Statement of Assets and Liabilities (Balance Sheet)
2. Statement of Operations (Income Statement)
3. Statement of Changes in Net Assets

6. Financial Highlights

## Investment Measurement

### Fair Value Requirement

**Substantially all investments at fair value:**
- Market prices for traded securities
- Model-based valuations for illiquid investments
- Changes recognized in statement of operations

### Fair Value Hierarchy

| Level | Description | Examples |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| **Level 1** | Quoted prices in active markets | NYSE-traded stocks |
| **Level 2** | Observable inputs | Corporate bonds, options |
| **Level 3** | Unobservable inputs | Private equity, VC investments |

## Investment Company Consolidation

### Non-Consolidation Principle

Investment companies generally do **NOT** consolidate their investees:
- Measure investments at fair value
- Do not apply equity method
- Apply to both controlling and non-controlling investments

### Exception—Operating Companies

If investment company has operating company subsidiaries that provide services:
- May consolidate operating subsidiary
- Investments in entities held for investment remain at fair value

### Retained Investment Company Accounting

When a parent consolidates an investment company subsidiary:
- Investment company retains its specialized accounting
- Parent reflects investment company investments at fair value
- Not consolidated line by line at parent level

## Practical Examples

### Example 1: Investment Purchase and Sale

**Purchase of Stock:**
```
Dr. Investments—Equity Securities    $1,000,000
    Cr. Cash                                     $1,000,000
(Purchase of 10,000 shares at $100)
```

**Fair Value Change:**
```
Stock price increases to $110

Dr. Investments—Equity Securities      $100,000
    Cr. Net Change in Unrealized Appreciation   $100,000
```

**Sale of Stock:**
```
Sold at $115 per share

Dr. Cash                             $1,150,000
    Cr. Investments—Equity Securities         $1,100,000
    Cr. Net Realized Gain on Investments         $50,000
(Realized gain: $1,150,000 - $1,000,000 cost = $150,000)
(Reversal of unrealized: $100,000)
(Net to realized: $50,000)
```

---

### Example 2: NAV Calculation

**Facts:**
- Total assets: $108,400,000
- Total liabilities: $4,500,000
- Shares outstanding: 10,000,000

**Calculation:**
```
Net Asset Value = (Total Assets - Total Liabilities) / Shares
NAV = ($108,400,000 - $4,500,000) / 10,000,000
NAV = $103,900,000 / 10,000,000
NAV = $10.39 per share
```

---

### Example 3: Capital Transactions

**Share Subscription (at NAV $10.39):**
```
Dr. Cash                             $1,039,000
    Cr. Paid-in Capital                       $1,039,000
(Issued 100,000 shares at $10.39)
```

**Share Redemption:**
```
Dr. Paid-in Capital                    $519,500
    Cr. Cash                                    $519,500
(Redeemed 50,000 shares at $10.39)
```

---

### Example 4: Distribution to Shareholders

**Dividend Declaration ($0.08/share):**
```
Dr. Accumulated Undistributed NII      $800,000
    Cr. Dividends Payable                       $800,000
(10,000,000 shares × $0.08)
```

**Capital Gains Distribution ($0.20/share):**
```
Dr. Accumulated Net Realized Gain    $2,000,000
    Cr. Capital Gains Distribution Payable    $2,000,000
(10,000,000 shares × $0.20)
```

## Private Fund Considerations

### Private Equity/Venture Capital

| Consideration | Guidance |
|---------------|----------|
| **Valuation** | Often Level 3; requires significant judgment |
| **Carried interest** | Allocation of profits to manager |
| **Committed capital** | Total investor commitments |
| **Drawdowns** | Capital calls from investors |
| **Distributions** | Return of capital and profits |

### Hedge Funds

| Consideration | Guidance |
|---------------|----------|
| **Master-feeder structure** | Consolidated or separate reporting |
| **Side pockets** | Illiquid investment segregation |
| **Gates** | Redemption limitations |
| **Lock-ups** | Initial investment holding periods |

### SEC Registration

| Fund Type | Registration | Primary Rules |
|-----------|--------------|---------------|
| **Mutual funds** | 1940 Act | Investment Company Act |
| **ETFs** | 1940 Act | Plus exemptive orders |
| **Closed-end funds** | 1940 Act | Investment Company Act |

| Requirement | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
