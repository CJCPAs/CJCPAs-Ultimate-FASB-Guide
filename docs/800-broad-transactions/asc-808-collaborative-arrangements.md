# ASC 808: Collaborative Arrangements

> Accounting for joint operating activities conducted without creating a separate legal entity.

## Overview

ASC 808 provides guidance on accounting for collaborative arrangements—contractual arrangements where two or more parties actively participate in a joint operating activity and share significant risks and rewards. Unlike joint ventures, collaborative arrangements do not involve creating a separate entity.

> **Core Principle:** Participants in collaborative arrangements should present the results of activities based on the nature of the arrangement and each participant's direct involvement, applying other applicable GAAP as appropriate.

---

## Scope

### Definition of Collaborative Arrangement

A contractual arrangement involving two or more parties that:
1. **Active participation** — All parties are actively involved in the activity
2. **Shared risks and rewards** — All parties are exposed to significant risks and rewards dependent on commercial success

### Common Examples

| Industry | Example |
|----------|---------|
| **Pharmaceutical** | Joint drug development agreements |
| **Technology** | Co-development of new products |
| **Entertainment** | Film co-production arrangements |
| **Oil & Gas** | Joint operating agreements |
| **Real Estate** | Co-development projects |
| **Aerospace** | Joint aircraft development |

### Exclusions

- Arrangements accounted for as equity method investments
- Joint ventures (separate legal entities)
- Arrangements within scope of other specific guidance

---

## Key Characteristics

### Active Participation

All parties must be:
- Involved in management decisions
- Directing activities
- Contributing resources (funding, IP, labor)

**Passive investors are NOT collaborative arrangement participants**

### Significant Risks and Rewards

Exposure to risk of:
- Not achieving technical milestones
- Not receiving regulatory approval
- Commercial failure
- Cost overruns

Sharing in rewards:
- Profit sharing
- Royalties
- Milestone payments based on success

---

## Accounting Framework

### General Principle

ASC 808 does **not** provide detailed measurement guidance. Instead:
1. Apply other applicable GAAP first
2. Present transactions based on their nature
3. Use reasonable judgment for classification

### Decision Framework

```
Step 1: Is the arrangement with a customer?
    Yes → Apply ASC 606 (Revenue)
    No → Continue to Step 2

Step 2: Does other GAAP apply (e.g., ASC 845)?
    Yes → Apply that guidance
    No → Apply policy consistently, similar to analogous transactions
```

### ASU 2018-18 Clarification

Transactions with collaborative partners that are **customers** should be accounted for under ASC 606 for those units of account.

---

## Presentation Requirements

### Income Statement

Determine appropriate presentation based on:
- Nature of each transaction
- Entity's role in the arrangement
- Applicable GAAP

| Transaction Type | Typical Presentation |
|-----------------|---------------------|
| Revenue from customer portion | Revenue |
| Cost sharing receipts | Reduce R&D expense |
| Cost sharing payments | R&D expense |
| Milestone payments | May be revenue or expense reduction |
| Royalties | Revenue or expense reduction |

### Balance Sheet

- Present assets/liabilities based on nature
- Receivables from partners
- Payables to partners
- May require separate line items if material

## Practical Examples

### Example 1: Pharmaceutical Co-Development

**Arrangement:**
- Company A and Company B jointly develop drug
- A contributes compound; B contributes clinical expertise
- Costs shared 60/40 (A/B)
- Profits shared 60/40 upon commercialization

**A's Accounting for $10M in Joint R&D Costs:**

A's share (60%): $6,000,000
```
Dr. R&D Expense                          $6,000,000
    Cr. Cash                                    $6,000,000
```

Cost reimbursement from B for A's services ($2M):
```
Dr. Cash                                 $2,000,000
    Cr. R&D Expense (reduction)                 $2,000,000
```
*Or present as other income if policy election*

### Example 2: Technology Co-Development

**Arrangement:**
- Tech Company and Manufacturer co-develop product
- Tech provides IP and engineering
- Manufacturer provides production capability
- Revenue sharing: 70/30

**Accounting for $5M Shared Development Costs:**

Tech Company's share: $3,500,000
```
Dr. R&D Expense                          $3,500,000
    Cr. Cash                                    $3,500,000
```

### Example 3: Collaborative Partner as Customer

**Arrangement:**
- Company A develops drug with Company B
- Company B has option to purchase finished product
- When B exercises option, B is a customer for that sale

**B's Purchase of $1M of Product:**

Company A's entry:
```
Dr. Accounts Receivable                  $1,000,000
    Cr. Revenue (ASC 606 applies)              $1,000,000
```

## Milestone Payments

### Types of Milestones

| Milestone Type | Example | Typical Treatment |
|----------------|---------|-------------------|
| **Development** | Completing Phase II trials | Reduce R&D expense (recipient) |
| **Regulatory** | FDA approval | May be revenue or expense reduction |
| **Commercial** | First commercial sale | Often revenue |
| **Sales-based** | Achieving $100M in sales | Revenue |

### Recipient Accounting

**$5M milestone received upon FDA approval:**
```
Dr. Cash                                 $5,000,000
    Cr. R&D Expense                            $5,000,000
```
*Or revenue if determined to be from customer*

### Payor Accounting

**$5M milestone payment made:**
```
Dr. R&D Expense (or intangible)          $5,000,000
    Cr. Cash                                    $5,000,000
```

## Royalties and Profit Sharing

### Royalty Receipts

When entity receives royalties from collaborative partner:
- Revenue if partner is customer
- Other income or expense reduction if partner is not customer

### Royalty Payments

When entity pays royalties to collaborative partner:
- Cost of sales (if product-related)
- R&D expense (if development-related)
- License expense

### Example—Profit Sharing

**Arrangement:** 50/50 profit split after costs
**Quarter Results:** Revenue $10M, Costs $4M, Profit $6M

**Each Party Records:**
```
Revenue (own sales or share): varies by arrangement
Cost of sales: varies
Settlement with partner: adjust to reach 50/50
```

## Disclosure Requirements

### Qualitative Disclosures

1. **Nature and purpose** of arrangement
2. **Rights and obligations** of each party
3. **Accounting policy** for arrangement

### Quantitative Disclosures

1. **Revenue and costs** recognized from arrangement
2. **Classification** in financial statements
3. **Receivables and payables** with partners

### Example Disclosure

> **Collaborative Arrangements:** The Company has entered into collaborative arrangements for the joint development and commercialization of pharmaceutical products. Under these arrangements, development costs are shared based on each party's proportionate interest, and profits from commercialization are shared based on contractual terms.
>
> For the year ended December 31, 20XX:
> - Research and development expenses include $X million of costs incurred under collaborative arrangements, net of $X million received from collaborative partners.
> - Revenue includes $X million for product sales to collaborative partners accounted for under ASC 606.

## ASU 2018-18: Key Clarifications

### Determining Customer Status

A collaborative partner is a customer if:
- Entity provides distinct goods/services
- Partner obtains control of those goods/services

### Unit of Account

Separate the customer portion from the collaboration portion:
- Customer transactions → ASC 606
- Collaboration transactions → ASC 808

### Presentation Guidance

When partner is NOT a customer and no other GAAP applies:
- Do NOT present as revenue from contracts with customers
- Present consistently with economic substance

---

## Private Company Considerations

### Prevalence

- Less common in private companies
- Often seen in:
  - Technology startups with strategic partners
  - Real estate development
  - Manufacturing joint efforts

### Practical Approach

- Document arrangement thoroughly
- Determine if partner is customer for any elements
- Apply consistent policy
- Focus on economic substance

---

## Common Audit Issues

1. **Customer determination** — Incorrectly identifying partner as customer
2. **Revenue classification** — Presenting collaboration receipts as revenue
3. **Gross vs. net** — Presenting shared costs on gross basis
4. **Milestone accounting** — Improper timing or classification
5. **Disclosure completeness** — Missing required information
6. **Policy consistency** — Changing treatment period to period
7. **Unit of account** — Not separating customer elements

---

## Comparison: Collaborative Arrangements vs. Joint Ventures

| Factor | Collaborative Arrangement | Joint Venture |
|--------|--------------------------|---------------|
| Legal entity | No separate entity | Separate legal entity |
| Accounting | ASC 808 framework | Equity method (ASC 323) |
| Control | Shared operating control | Shared equity ownership |
| Balance sheet | Individual assets/liabilities | Investment account |
| Income statement | Based on nature of transactions | Equity in earnings |
