# ASC 400-499: Liabilities

> Recognition, measurement, and presentation of liabilities including debt, leases, and contingencies.

## Topic Overview

The 400 series covers accounting for liabilities including debt arrangements, leases, commitments, and contingencies.

## Key Standards

### ASC 842: Leases

📌 **Major Standard — Impacts virtually all entities with leases**

See detailed guide: [ASC 842: Leases](asc-842-leases.md)

**Overview:**

ASC 842 requires lessees to recognize most leases on the balance sheet as a right-of-use (ROU) asset and lease liability.

**Scope:**
- Property, plant, and equipment leases
- Includes embedded leases in service contracts
- **Excludes:** Leases of intangible assets, inventory, assets under construction

**Key Changes from ASC 840:**
- Lessees recognize virtually all leases on balance sheet
- New lease classification criteria (finance vs. operating)
- Enhanced disclosure requirements

**Lessee Accounting:**

| Lease Type | Balance Sheet | Income Statement |
|------------|---------------|------------------|
| **Finance Lease** | ROU Asset + Lease Liability | Interest expense + Amortization (front-loaded) |
| **Operating Lease** | ROU Asset + Lease Liability | Single lease expense (straight-line) |

**Effective Dates:**

| Entity Type | Effective Date |
|-------------|----------------|
| Public entities | Fiscal years beginning after 12/15/2018 |
| Private companies | Fiscal years beginning after 12/15/2021 |

### ASC 450: Contingencies

📌 **Major Standard — Loss and gain contingency accounting**

See detailed guide: [ASC 450: Contingencies](asc-450-contingencies.md)

**Loss Contingencies:**

| Likelihood | Accounting Treatment |
|------------|---------------------|
| **Probable** (likely to occur) | Accrue if estimable; disclose if not estimable |
| **Reasonably Possible** | Disclose only |
| **Remote** | No disclosure required (except guarantees) |

**Plain English:**
- **Probable** = More likely than not to happen
- **Reasonably Possible** = Could happen
- **Remote** = Very unlikely

**Gain Contingencies:**
- Never accrue gains until realized
- May disclose if probable, but avoid misleading implications

### ASC 470: Debt

📌 **Major Standard — Debt accounting, modifications, and convertibles**

See detailed guide: [ASC 470: Debt](asc-470-debt.md)

**Debt Modifications vs. Extinguishments:**

| Criterion | Modification | Extinguishment |
|-----------|--------------|----------------|
| Cash flow change | < 10% | ≥ 10% |
| Treatment | Prospective adjustment | Gain/loss recognition |

**10% Test:** Compare present value of cash flows under new terms vs. old terms

**Debt Issuance Costs:**
- Present as direct deduction from debt carrying amount
- Amortize using effective interest method

**Convertible Debt (ASU 2020-06):**
- Simplified model: Account for most convertibles as single liability
- Removes beneficial conversion feature and cash conversion models
- Effective for public entities: 2022; private entities: 2024

### ASC 480: Distinguishing Liabilities from Equity

**Mandatorily Redeemable Instruments:**
- Instruments that embody unconditional obligation to redeem = Liability
- Instruments redeemable at holder's option = Liability or equity (depends on terms)

**Mezzanine Equity:**
- Redeemable outside issuer's control but not mandatorily redeemable
- Present between liabilities and equity

## Practical Examples

### Operating Lease Recognition (Lessee)

**Scenario:** Company signs 5-year building lease. Annual payments of $100,000 at year-end. Discount rate: 5%.

**At Lease Commencement:**

Present value of lease payments: $432,948

```
Dr. Right-of-Use Asset           $432,948
    Cr. Lease Liability                  $432,948
```

**Year 1 — Monthly Expense Recognition:**

Annual lease expense = $100,000 / 12 = $8,333/month (straight-line)

Interest for Year 1: $432,948 × 5% = $21,647
Principal: $100,000 - $21,647 = $78,353

### Loss Contingency Accrual

**Scenario:** Company is defendant in lawsuit. Legal counsel believes it's probable the company will lose and estimates damages of $500,000.

**Journal Entry:**
```
Dr. Litigation Loss Expense      $500,000
    Cr. Litigation Liability             $500,000
```

**Disclosure Required:** Nature of contingency, amount accrued

## Private Company Considerations

| Topic | PCC Alternative |
|-------|-----------------|
| **Leases** | Can use risk-free rate instead of incremental borrowing rate |
| **Common Control Leases** | Written terms govern, not legal enforceability (ASU 2023-01) |
| **Variable Interest Entities** | Alternative consolidation guidance |

### Practical Expedients for Leases

Private companies may elect:
- **Portfolio approach** for similar leases
- **Risk-free discount rate** instead of incremental borrowing rate
- **Short-term lease exemption** (≤12 months)
- **Combine lease and non-lease components**

## Common Audit Issues

1. **Lease Identification** — Missing embedded leases in service contracts
2. **Contingency Accruals** — Underestimating probable losses
3. **Debt Covenants** — Improper classification when in violation
4. **Related-Party Leases** — Terms not at arm's length
