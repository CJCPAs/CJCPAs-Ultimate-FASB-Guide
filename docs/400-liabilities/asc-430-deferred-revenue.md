# ASC 430: Deferred Revenue

> Accounting for contract liabilities and unearned revenue received before performance.

## Overview

ASC 430 addresses deferred revenue (also called unearned revenue or contract liabilities)—amounts received from customers before the entity satisfies its performance obligations. While ASC 430 provides general guidance, most deferred revenue accounting now follows ASC 606 (Revenue from Contracts with Customers) which provides comprehensive guidance on when to recognize revenue.

**Core Principle:**
> "Deferred revenue represents an entity's obligation to transfer goods or services to a customer for which consideration has already been received. Revenue is recognized when (or as) the performance obligation is satisfied."

---

## Scope

### Applies To:
- Advance payments from customers
- Prepaid services
- Subscription fees received in advance
- Deposits and retainers
- Gift cards and loyalty programs
- Unearned revenue from contracts

### Relationship to ASC 606

Most deferred revenue follows ASC 606 guidance:
- Recognition when performance obligation satisfied
- Contract liability terminology
- Five-step revenue model

---

## Key Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Deferred Revenue** | Liability for consideration received before performance |
| **Contract Liability** | Obligation to transfer goods/services when consideration received (ASC 606 term) |
| **Unearned Revenue** | Another term for deferred revenue |
| **Performance Obligation** | Promise to transfer distinct good or service |
| **Customer Deposit** | Payment received before goods/services specified |
| **Breakage** | Portion of prepaid amounts customer will not use |

---

## Recognition and Measurement

### Initial Recognition

When consideration is received before performance:

```
Dr. Cash                             $12,000
    Cr. Deferred Revenue (Contract Liability)  $12,000
(Received annual subscription payment in advance)
```

### Revenue Recognition

When (or as) performance obligation is satisfied:

**Over Time Recognition:**
```
Dr. Deferred Revenue                  $1,000
    Cr. Revenue                               $1,000
(Monthly recognition of annual subscription)
```

**Point in Time Recognition:**
```
Dr. Deferred Revenue                  $5,000
    Cr. Revenue                               $5,000
(Product delivered)
```

## Common Deferred Revenue Scenarios

### 1. Subscription Services

**Facts:**
- Customer pays $1,200 for 12-month subscription
- Payment received January 1

**At Payment:**
```
Dr. Cash                              $1,200
    Cr. Deferred Revenue                      $1,200
```

**Monthly Recognition:**
```
Dr. Deferred Revenue                    $100
    Cr. Subscription Revenue                    $100
```

---

### 2. Gift Cards

**Facts:**
- Gift cards sold: $50,000
- Redeemed during period: $35,000
- Estimated breakage (to be recognized): $3,000

**Sale of Gift Cards:**
```
Dr. Cash                             $50,000
    Cr. Gift Card Liability (Deferred Revenue)  $50,000
```

**Redemption:**
```
Dr. Gift Card Liability              $35,000
    Cr. Revenue                              $35,000
```

**Breakage Recognition (if pattern estimable):**
```
Dr. Gift Card Liability               $3,000
    Cr. Breakage Revenue                      $3,000
```

### Gift Card Breakage Methods

| Method | Description | When to Use |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| **Proportional** | Recognize in proportion to redemptions | Expected redemption pattern known |
| **Remote** | Recognize when likelihood of redemption is remote | Cannot estimate pattern |
| **Escheatment** | Remit to state as unclaimed property | State law requires |

---

### 3. Loyalty Programs

**Facts:**
- Customer earns 1,000 points on $1,000 purchase
- Points worth $50 (separate performance obligation)
- Transaction price allocation based on relative standalone selling prices

**At Sale:**
```
Dr. Cash                              $1,000
    Cr. Revenue (goods)                         $952
    Cr. Deferred Revenue (points)                $48
(Allocation based on relative SSP)
```

**At Point Redemption:**
```
Dr. Deferred Revenue                    $48
    Cr. Revenue                                 $48
```

---

### 4. Prepaid Maintenance Contracts

**Facts:**
- 3-year maintenance contract
- Customer pays $9,000 upfront
- Services provided ratably

**At Payment:**
```
Dr. Cash                              $9,000
    Cr. Deferred Revenue                      $9,000
```

**Monthly Recognition (36 months):**
```
Dr. Deferred Revenue                    $250
    Cr. Service Revenue                         $250
```

---

### 5. Software-as-a-Service (SaaS)

**Facts:**
- Annual SaaS contract: $24,000
- Paid quarterly in advance: $6,000
- Revenue recognized ratably over service period

**Quarterly Payment Received:**
```
Dr. Cash                              $6,000
    Cr. Deferred Revenue                      $6,000
```

**Monthly Recognition:**
```
Dr. Deferred Revenue                  $2,000
    Cr. SaaS Revenue                          $2,000
```

---

### 6. Customer Deposits (Refundable)

**Facts:**
- Deposit received: $5,000
- Refundable if customer cancels before delivery

**At Receipt:**
```
Dr. Cash                              $5,000
    Cr. Customer Deposits (Liability)         $5,000
```

**At Delivery (non-refundable):**
```
Dr. Customer Deposits                 $5,000
    Cr. Deferred Revenue (or Revenue)         $5,000
```

## Classification and Presentation

### Balance Sheet Classification

| Timeframe | Classification |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Within 12 months** | Current liability |
| **Beyond 12 months** | Noncurrent liability |

### Balance Sheet Presentation

```
CURRENT LIABILITIES
  Accounts payable                    $  500,000
  Accrued expenses                       200,000
  Deferred revenue (contract liabilities) 150,000
  Current portion of long-term debt      100,000
                                      ----------
Total current liabilities               $950,000

NONCURRENT LIABILITIES
  Long-term debt                      $1,000,000
  Deferred revenue, net of current portion  75,000
```

## Practical Examples

### Example 1: Multiple Performance Obligations

**Facts:**
- Sell equipment + 2-year maintenance for $15,000
- Equipment SSP: $12,000
- Maintenance SSP: $4,000
- Total SSP: $16,000

**Transaction Price Allocation:**
- Equipment: $15,000 × ($12,000 / $16,000) = $11,250
- Maintenance: $15,000 × ($4,000 / $16,000) = $3,750

**At Contract Inception (Equipment Delivered):**
```
Dr. Cash                             $15,000
    Cr. Revenue—Equipment                    $11,250
    Cr. Deferred Revenue—Maintenance          $3,750
```

**Monthly Maintenance Recognition (24 months):**
```
Dr. Deferred Revenue                   $156
    Cr. Revenue—Maintenance                    $156
```

---

### Example 2: Nonrefundable Upfront Fees

**Facts:**
- Health club charges $500 initiation fee (nonrefundable)
- Monthly membership: $50
- Initiation fee provides no distinct service

**Analysis:** Initiation fee is advance payment for future services—recognize over expected membership period (assume 2 years).

**At Sign-Up:**
```
Dr. Cash                               $550
    Cr. Deferred Revenue                       $500
    Cr. Membership Revenue                      $50
```

**Monthly (Initiation Fee):**
```
Dr. Deferred Revenue                   $21
    Cr. Membership Revenue                      $21
($500 / 24 months)
```

---

### Example 3: Contract Liability Rollforward

```
CONTRACT LIABILITY (DEFERRED REVENUE) ROLLFORWARD
For the Year Ended December 31, 20X1

Beginning balance                     $   500,000
Revenue recognized from beginning balance (400,000)
Increases from new contracts            1,200,000
Revenue recognized from new contracts    (800,000)
                                      -----------
Ending balance                        $   500,000
```

## Breakage

### Overview

Breakage occurs when customers prepay but never fully use their rights (e.g., unused gift cards, expired points).

### Recognition Methods

**If Breakage Can Be Estimated:**
- Recognize breakage proportionately with redemptions
- Pattern of rights exercise

**If Breakage Cannot Be Estimated:**
- Recognize when likelihood of redemption is remote
- May be when gift card expires

### Example

**Facts:**
- Gift cards sold: $100,000
- Estimated breakage: 8%
- Current period redemptions: 25% of total expected

**Breakage Recognition:**
```
Breakage amount: $100,000 × 8% = $8,000
Current period breakage: $8,000 × 25% = $2,000

Dr. Gift Card Liability               $2,000
    Cr. Breakage Revenue                      $2,000
```

## Private Company Considerations

### Common Issues

| Issue | Consideration |
|-------|---------------|
| **Simple contracts** | May have single performance obligation |
| **Subscription models** | Straightforward ratable recognition |
| **Deposits** | Ensure proper classification |
| **Customer advances** | Document terms clearly |

### Practical Approaches

| Area | Simplification |
|------|----------------|
| **Immaterial amounts** | May not require extensive analysis |
| **Standard terms** | Apply consistent policies |
| **Short-term contracts** | Often recognized at point in time |

## Disclosure Requirements

### ASC 606 Contract Liability Disclosures

| Item | Disclosure |
|------|------------|
| **Opening/closing balances** | Contract liability amounts |
| **Revenue recognized** | From beginning contract liabilities |
| **Significant changes** | Explanations for material changes |
| **Performance obligations** | Description and timing |
| **Remaining obligations** | For contracts > 1 year |

### Example Disclosure

```
NOTE X: DEFERRED REVENUE (CONTRACT LIABILITIES)

Contract liabilities represent advance payments from customers for
goods and services not yet delivered. Contract liabilities were
$500,000 and $400,000 at December 31, 20X1 and 20X0, respectively.
Revenue recognized during 20X1 that was included in contract
liabilities at January 1, 20X1 was $380,000.
```
