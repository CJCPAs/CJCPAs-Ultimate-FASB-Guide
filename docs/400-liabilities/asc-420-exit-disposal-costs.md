# ASC 420: Exit or Disposal Cost Obligations

> Accounting for costs associated with restructuring, plant closings, and employee terminations.

## Overview

ASC 420 addresses the recognition and measurement of liabilities for costs associated with exit or disposal activities. These activities include restructurings, plant closings, and employee terminations. The key principle is that a liability is recognized when incurred, not when management commits to a plan.

**Core Principle:**
> "A liability for a cost associated with an exit or disposal activity shall be recognized when the liability is incurred—not at the commitment date when a plan is approved."

---

## Scope

### Applies To:
- Costs associated with exit or disposal activities, including:
  - Involuntary employee termination benefits (one-time)
  - Costs to terminate a contract (other than a lease)
  - Other associated costs (relocation, consolidation)
- Facility closings
- Restructuring activities

### Does NOT Apply To:
- Employee termination benefits under ongoing benefit arrangements (ASC 712)
- Lease termination costs (ASC 842)
- Costs to exit activities in a business combination (ASC 805)
- Discontinued operations (ASC 205-20)
- Asset impairments (ASC 360)

---

## Key Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Exit Activity** | Includes restructuring and discontinuing a product line, closing a facility |
| **Disposal Activity** | Disposing of long-lived assets (group) |
| **One-Time Termination Benefits** | Benefits paid as result of one-time event (not ongoing plan) |
| **Contract Termination Costs** | Costs to terminate contract before end of term |
| **Commitment Date** | Date management approves plan (NOT recognition date) |
| **Liability Incurred Date** | Date obligation exists (recognition date) |

---

## When Is a Liability Incurred?

### Recognition Timing by Cost Type

| Cost Type | When Liability Is Incurred |
|-----------|---------------------------|
| **One-time termination benefits** | When communicated to employees (unless future service required) |
| **Contract termination costs** | When contract is terminated OR entity ceases using right under contract |
| **Other exit costs** | When liability is incurred (goods/services received) |

### Key Distinction

**NOT the commitment date:** A liability is NOT recognized when:
- Management approves plan
- Board approves plan
- Plan is announced to public

**Rather, the incurred date:** When an obligating event occurs.

---

## One-Time Employee Termination Benefits

### Communication Requirement

Liability recognized when the plan is **communicated** to employees, IF:

1. Management commits to termination plan
2. Plan identifies the number, locations, and job functions of terminated employees
3. Plan establishes termination benefits in sufficient detail
4. Actions required to complete the plan indicate significant changes are unlikely

### Future Service Required

If employees must work beyond minimum retention period to receive benefits:

| Situation | Recognition |
|-----------|-------------|
| **No future service required** | Full liability at communication date |
| **Future service required** | Ratably over service period |

### Measurement

**At communication date:** Fair value of liability

For termination benefits paid over time:
- Present value if timing extends beyond one year

---

## Journal Entries—Termination Benefits

### No Future Service Required

**At Communication Date:**
```
Dr. Restructuring Expense            $500,000
    Cr. Accrued Termination Benefits         $500,000
```

### Future Service Required (Ratably)

**Facts:**
- Total benefits: $300,000
- Service period: 6 months

**Each Month (over 6 months):**
```
Dr. Restructuring Expense             $50,000
    Cr. Accrued Termination Benefits          $50,000
```

### Payment

**When Benefits Paid:**
```
Dr. Accrued Termination Benefits     $500,000
    Cr. Cash                                  $500,000
```

---

## Contract Termination Costs

### Recognition

Recognize when:
- **Contract is terminated** under its terms, OR
- **Entity ceases using** the right conveyed by the contract

### Measurement

**At fair value**, which is the cost to terminate or settle:

| Element | Measurement |
|---------|-------------|
| **Termination penalty** | Contractual amount |
| **Remaining payments** | PV of payments after cease-use date |
| **Less sublease income** | Reduce by expected sublease receipts |

### Journal Entry

**Contract Termination:**
```
Dr. Contract Termination Expense     $150,000
    Cr. Accrued Contract Termination Costs   $150,000
```

---

## Other Associated Costs

### Types of Other Costs

| Cost | Treatment |
|------|-----------|
| **Relocation costs** | Recognize when incurred |
| **Employee retraining** | Recognize when incurred |
| **Consolidation costs** | Recognize when incurred |
| **Consulting fees** | Recognize as services received |

### Key Principle

These costs are recognized in the period incurred—not at plan approval:

```
Dr. Restructuring Expense            $75,000
    Cr. Cash/Accounts Payable                $75,000
(Recognized when services received or goods used)
```

## Practical Examples

### Example 1: Plant Closing with Termination Benefits

**Facts:**
- December 1: Management commits to plan, identifies 50 employees
- December 15: Plan communicated to employees
- Termination benefits: $1,000,000
- No future service required
- Plant closes: March 31

**December 15 (Communication Date):**
```
Dr. Restructuring Expense          $1,000,000
    Cr. Accrued Termination Benefits       $1,000,000
```

**Note:** NOT recognized on December 1 (commitment date)

---

### Example 2: Termination with Future Service

**Facts:**
- January 1: Communicate plan requiring 4 months service
- Total termination benefits: $400,000
- Employees must work through April 30

**Each Month (January through April):**
```
Dr. Restructuring Expense           $100,000
    Cr. Accrued Termination Benefits        $100,000
```

---

### Example 3: Contract Termination

**Facts:**
- 5-year contract for IT services (2 years remaining)
- Annual payments: $120,000
- Termination penalty: $50,000
- Cease use date: June 30

**At Cease Use Date (June 30):**

Liability calculation:
- Remaining payments: $240,000 (2 years × $120,000)
- PV of remaining payments (assume present value): $220,000
- Plus termination penalty: $50,000
- Total: $270,000

```
Dr. Contract Termination Expense     $270,000
    Cr. Accrued Contract Costs               $270,000
```

---

### Example 4: Comprehensive Restructuring

**Facts (all communicated/incurred in December):**
- Termination benefits (no future service): $800,000
- Contract termination costs: $200,000
- Relocation costs (incurred): $50,000
- Asset impairments: $300,000

**Entries:**

**Termination Benefits:**
```
Dr. Restructuring Expense—Termination  $800,000
    Cr. Accrued Termination Benefits          $800,000
```

**Contract Termination:**
```
Dr. Restructuring Expense—Contract     $200,000
    Cr. Accrued Contract Costs                $200,000
```

**Relocation (as incurred):**
```
Dr. Restructuring Expense—Relocation    $50,000
    Cr. Cash/Payables                          $50,000
```

**Asset Impairment (per ASC 360):**
```
Dr. Impairment Loss                    $300,000
    Cr. Accumulated Depreciation              $300,000
```

## Changes in Estimates

### Subsequent Changes

Changes in the liability are recognized in the period of change:

**Increase in Estimate:**
```
Dr. Restructuring Expense             $75,000
    Cr. Accrued Restructuring Costs           $75,000
```

**Decrease in Estimate:**
```
Dr. Accrued Restructuring Costs       $40,000
    Cr. Restructuring Expense                  $40,000
(Or credit to income if original expense in prior period)
```

## Presentation and Classification

### Income Statement

| Method | Presentation |
|--------|--------------|
| **Separate line item** | "Restructuring charges" |
| **By function** | Include in respective expense categories |
| **Within operating** | Generally operating expense |

### Balance Sheet

| Timeframe | Classification |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Due within 1 year** | Current liability |
| **Due beyond 1 year** | Noncurrent liability |

### Required Disclosures

| Item | Disclosure |
|------|------------|
| **Description** | Nature and reasons for exit/disposal activity |
| **Expected completion** | Date expected to be substantially complete |
| **Costs by category** | Termination, contract, other |

## Private Company Considerations

### Common Restructuring Situations

| Situation | Consideration |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Plant consolidation** | Identify all cost categories |
| **Workforce reduction** | Communication is key trigger |
| **Owner retirements** | May trigger restructuring |

### Documentation Requirements

- Board/management approval (commitment date)
- Communication to employees (recognition date)
- Detailed cost estimates and assumptions
- Support for future service determinations

## Common Audit Issues

### Timing

| Issue | Risk |
|-------|------|
| **Premature recognition** | Recording at commitment vs. incurred date |
| **Incomplete identification** | Missing costs or activities |
| **Communication evidence** | Verifying employee notification |

### Measurement

| Issue | Consideration |
|-------|---------------|
| **Estimate accuracy** | Reasonableness of projected costs |
| **Present value** | Appropriate discount rate |
| **Subsequent changes** | Proper adjustment treatment |

### Completeness

| Area | Audit Focus |
|------|-------------|
| **All locations** | Ensure all facilities identified |
| **All cost types** | Termination, contract, other |
| **Subsequent events** | Changes after balance sheet date |

## Comparison: ASC 420 vs. Other Standards

| Standard | Applies To |
|----------|------------|
| **ASC 420** | One-time exit/disposal costs |
| **ASC 712** | Ongoing termination benefit plans |
| **ASC 715** | Pension curtailments/settlements |
| **ASC 842** | Lease terminations |
| **ASC 360** | Asset impairments |
| **ASC 205-20** | Discontinued operations |
